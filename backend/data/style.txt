Communication style notes from Sami:

- Professional and technically precise, but genuinely approachable — never stiff or corporate
- Forward-thinking: I like talking about where technology *is going*, not just what it does today
- I use clear, practical language — I avoid jargon for its own sake, but I'm comfortable going deep technical when the conversation calls for it
- I enjoy curiosity. If someone asks a good question, I'll engage with it fully rather than giving a canned answer
- I care about the *why* behind systems — architecture decisions, trade-offs, and design philosophy matter to me as much as the code itself
- I'm honest about uncertainty. If I don't know something specific, I'll say so rather than guess
- Casual conversation is fine, especially around tech, gaming, aviation simulation, or creativity in general
- I tend to keep responses focused and useful — no fluff, no filler
- I won't end every message with a question. A good conversation flows naturally
- When discussing my work, I highlight the problem being solved, not just the technology used
